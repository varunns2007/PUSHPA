1. Executive Summary & Core Problem Statement

Forests across critical ecological hotspots such as the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve, Anamalai Range, and Periyar Corridor face unprecedented threats from illegal logging, organized timber poaching (e.g., Red Sanders, Rosewood, and Teak), and clandestine transit smuggling. Traditional forest protection methods rely heavily on manual foot patrols, delayed physical reports, and isolated boundary checkpoints, resulting in significant operational blind spots:

Timber Smuggling Value

< 8.5%

PUSHPA (Predictive Unified System for Forest Protection & Anti-Smuggling) overcomes these challenges by delivering an end-to-end, multi-sensor intelligence fusion platform. By marrying spaceborne optical/infrared multi-spectral imagery with ground telemetry, legal transit databases, and real-time anomaly detection, PUSHPA condenses the detection-to-interdiction loop from weeks down to minutes.

3. Copernicus Sentinel-2 Multi-Spectral Pipeline & NDVI Processing

PUSHPA integrates with the European Space Agency's (ESA) Copernicus Sentinel-2 constellation (Sentinel-2A & Sentinel-2B), providing multi-spectral imagery with a 5-day orbital revisit frequency and a spatial resolution of up to 10 meters per pixel.

Spectral Bands Ingested

Band 4 (B04) — Red
665 nm
10m resolution. Absorbed by chlorophyll for photosynthesis. High reflectance indicates bare earth / cleared timber.
Band 8 (B08) — NIR
842 nm
10m resolution. Strongly reflected by healthy leaf mesophyll cell structure. Drops sharply upon tree felling.
SCL (Scene Classification)
Level-2A
Used for cloud masking, cirrus filtering, topographic cloud shadow removal, and water body isolation.

Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) Mathematics

Live vegetation absorbs red light while reflecting near-infrared radiation. PUSHPA calculates the NDVI per pixel using normalized floating-point matrices:

NDVI = (B08 - B04) / (B08 + B04 + ε)
Where ε = 1e-6 prevents zero-division errors over deep shadow / water surfaces.

Backend implementation from app/satellite/ndvi.py:

import numpy as np

def compute_ndvi(b04: np.ndarray, b08: np.ndarray) -> np.ndarray:
    # Convert raw digital numbers to 32-bit floating point
    b04_f = b04.astype(np.float32)
    b08_f = b08.astype(np.float32)
    denominator = b08_f + b04_f
    # Apply zero-safe division mask
    ndvi = np.where(denominator != 0, (b08_f - b04_f) / denominator, 0.0)
    # Clip strict physical range [-1.0, 1.0]
    return np.clip(ndvi, -1.0, 1.0)

Atmospheric Cloud & Shadow Masking

To eliminate false positive alerts caused by monsoonal cloud cover or terrain shadows, the pipeline applies a multi-stage mask:

  • SCL Filtering: Pixels flagged as 3 (Cloud Shadows), 8 (Cloud Medium Prob), 9 (Cloud High Prob), or 10 (Thin Cirrus) are discarded and interpolated from the nearest prior valid observation.
  • Temporal Smoothing: Sliding-window median filters verify that a detected drop persists across consecutive satellite overpasses before triggering high-level alerts.

4. Vegetation Density Stratification & Bi-Temporal Vectorization

PUSHPA categorizes forest zones into four distinct density tiers based on calibrated radiometric thresholds, enabling rangers to identify not only total clear-cuts but also stealth degradation (thinning of mature trees under dense canopy).

Canopy Stratification Tier NDVI Range Ecological Characteristic Display Palette
Non-Vegetation / Cleared < 0.20 Bare soil, paved roads, recent clearings, rocky outcrops. Crimson (#dc2626)
Sparse Canopy 0.20 – 0.45 Grasslands, scrub forest, severely degraded timber zones. Amber (#f59e0b)
Moderate Canopy 0.45 – 0.70 Deciduous forest, secondary growth, plantation buffers. Lime Green (#84cc16)
Dense / Primary Canopy > 0.70 Virgin rainforest, high-density teak/rosewood reserves. Deep Emerald (#059669)

Bi-Temporal Change Detection & Vectorization Algorithm

To extract actionable geographic boundaries for field rangers, PUSHPA compares baseline imagery ($T_0$) with current observations ($T_1$):

Delta NDVI Mathematical Formulation
$$\Delta NDVI = NDVI_{after} - NDVI_{before}$$ A pixel is flagged as Severe Loss if $\Delta NDVI \le -0.30$ and $NDVI_{before} \ge 0.50$ (ensuring seasonal grass drying is not confused with mature tree felling).
1. Morphological Filtering
A $3 imes3$ morphological kernel performs cv2.morphologyEx(mask, cv2.MORPH_OPEN) to remove single-pixel sensor noise, followed by dilation to bridge fragmented clear-cut clusters.
2. Contour Vectorization
cv2.findContours(mask, cv2.RETR_EXTERNAL) extracts contiguous boundaries. Contours with an area ≥ 0.5 hectares are projected to WGS84 GeoJSON polygons (e.g. CHG_POLY_001).

Extracted change polygon metadata attributes include:

  • polygon_id: Unique identifier (e.g. CHG_POLY_001).
  • area_ha: Geographic surface area in hectares (calculated via EPSG:3857 planar projection).
  • vegetation_drop_pct: Mean canopy density decline within polygon bounds (e.g. -59.2%).
  • centroid: Latitude / Longitude coordinates for direct GPS waypoint navigation.
  • estimated_biomass_loss_tons: Estimated metric tonnage of felled timber calculated from canopy index.

Haversine Spatial Proximity Formulation

The engine computes the spherical distance between moving transport vehicles and freshly vectorized clearing polygons:

d = 2r · arcsin(√[sin²(Δlat/2) + cos(lat1)·cos(lat2)·sin²(Δlng/2)])

1. Arboreal Bio-Acoustic IoT Sensor Grid ("Forest Ears")

Solar-powered, weatherproof acoustic sensor nodes deployed in high-value tree canopies (every 500m in protected sandalwood/rosewood groves) running Edge TinyML Audio Classifiers (TensorFlow Lite / Edge Impulse):

  • Acoustic Fingerprint Detection: Continuously analyzes acoustic spectrums (120 Hz – 8 kHz) to detect the two-stroke engine harmonics of chainsaws, handsaws, tree-falling shockwaves, heavy diesel engine rumbles, and gunshots.
  • Sub-Second Edge Inference: Runs on ultra-low-power microcontrollers (Nordic nRF5340 / ESP32-S3 + MEMS microphone) consuming < 15mW, waking up only upon acoustic threshold trigger.
  • LoRaWAN Long-Range Uplink: Transmits acoustic alert packets with precise sensor GPS ID over a 15 km forest mesh network to base stations, achieving detection-to-alert latency of under 3 seconds.

2. Autonomous Tactical Drone Interception Fleet (UAV First-Response)

Deploying automated drone nesting stations (solar-powered weatherproof hangars) positioned across strategic forest ridges:

  • Instant Autonomous Scramble: When an acoustic sensor detects a chainsaw or the PUSHPA engine flags an off-route vehicle, the nearest drone launches autonomously within 45 seconds without requiring manual pilot setup.
  • Dual Optical / FLIR Thermal Payload: Penetrates tree canopy cover at night to track thermal signatures of poachers, woodcutters, campfires, and running truck engines.
  • Automated Target Tracking & Spotlight: Computer vision tracking locks onto fleeing vehicles, transmitting live HD video and GPS coordinates to the command center while illuminating the suspect with a 10,000-lumen strobe.

8. Real-Time Smuggling Prevention Architecture (Cont.)

3. Smart Corridors: ANPR, Dynamic Weighbridges & RFID Stump Tags

Preventing smuggled logs from exiting forest boundaries into commercial timber markets requires automated physical choke-points:

ANPR Optical Gates
High-speed ANPR cameras scan vehicle license plates at entry/exit points, querying the PUSHPA database in < 200ms. Unpermitted trucks trigger automated pneumatic spike barriers.
Weigh-in-Motion (WIM)
Sensors embedded in road asphalt weigh moving trucks without stopping. Density anomalies (e.g. hollowed-out cargo concealing high-density red sanders) trigger mandatory physical inspection bays.
RFID Timber Stump Nails
Forest departments tag legally harvestable trees with tamper-evident RFID/NFC tree-stump nails. Felled logs without valid cryptographic RFID tags are automatically seized at checkpoint scanners.

4. Blockchain-Backed Timber Transit Passport & Chain-of-Custody

Paper transit permits are notoriously vulnerable to counterfeiting, reuse, and bribery. PUSHPA integrates a distributed ledger (Hyperledger Fabric / Polygon Private Enterprise):

  • Cryptographic Digital Transit Passports: Every legal timber consignment receives a time-locked, geofenced, cryptographically signed NFT/Permit token containing harvest GPS, species genetics, log count, and destination mill.
  • Single-Use Burn Mechanism: Once a vehicle passes the final destination checkpost, the smart contract automatically burns the permit token, mathematically preventing permit reuse for multiple smuggling runs.
  • DNA & Isotope Timber Verification: Logs can be cross-verified against regional wood isotopic databases to ensure claimed timber origin matches genuine provenance.

5. Graph-Based Predictive Escape Route Forecasting & Chokepoint Interdiction

When an unauthorized vehicle is detected deep inside the forest, PUSHPA's Predictive Escape Engine executes a Dijkstra / Graph Neural Network (GNN) traversal across the topological forest road network:

Tactical Interdiction Vector Calculation
The algorithm computes the 3 most probable exit gates based on current vehicle heading, road gradient, unpaved trail passability, and historical smuggling escape routes. It calculates the Time-to-Exit ($T_{exit}$) versus the Ranger Interception Time ($T_{ranger}$) and automatically routes patrol jeeps to the optimal bottleneck chokepoint before the smuggler arrives.

10. Ethical Governance, Legal Admissibility & Operational Deployment

Deploying artificial intelligence and geospatial surveillance in sensitive biosphere reserves requires rigorous adherence to ethical standards, statutory environmental laws, and tribal community rights.

1. Human-in-the-Loop Advisory Boundary

Statutory Legal Safeguard
PUSHPA is strictly an intelligence prioritization and decision-support system. The software NEVER autonomously declares an event illegal, issues legal fines, or executes automated punitive enforcement. All alerts are categorized as "Potential Suspicious Activity / Investigation Required", ensuring that a qualified Forest Range Officer (FRO) or Divisional Forest Officer (DFO) performs physical verification before legal proceedings begin under the Wildlife Protection Act and Indian Forest Act.

2. Privacy & Indigenous Community Rights (FRA Compliance)

  • Tribal Foraging Protection: Algorithms are calibrated to distinguish between traditional non-timber forest produce (NTFP) collection by indigenous forest dwellers (which produces negligible canopy deltas) and commercial industrial clear-felling.
  • Privacy Preservation: Satellite optical processing occurs at 10m ground resolution, inherently preserving individual human privacy while resolving tree canopies and vehicular assets.
